## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rugged, dependable spirit of authentic East Javanese volcanic expeditions. Crafted for outdoor travelers seeking raw encounters with Mount Ijen’s electric-blue flames and sulphur trails, the visual direction merges tactical local expertise with polished hospitality. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an earthy, tactile minimalism—completely stripped of artificial neon glows, generic SaaS glassmorphism, or tech-centric styling. Instead, the interface relies on tactile volcanic tones, warm morning mist neutrals, and sunburst accents reminiscent of dawn breaks over the caldera rim.

Key brand tenets:
- **Grounded & Sturdy:** Interfaces project the mechanical reliability of classic 4x4 Land Cruisers navigating nocturnal mountain passes.
- **Warm & Trustworthy:** Friendly local hospitality counterbalances high-adventure terrains, putting travelers instantly at ease during safety briefings, booking procedures, and logistics planning.
- **Editorial Authenticity:** Visuals center real field photography—charcoal volcanic dust, mossy highland switchbacks, steaming turquoise caldera lakes, and the weathered gear of genuine local drivers.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the transition from midnight volcanic ascents to golden sunrise overlooking East Java's highland rainforests:

- **Primary (`#2E3E32`, Natural Deep Olive):** Grounding and serene. Used for primary branding elements, core navigational bars, prominent titles, and key structure containers. Represents highland foliage and sturdy canvas expedition gear.
- **Secondary (`#D9822B`, Sunrise Amber):** High-energy, warm golden accent representing the caldera dawn. Reserved strictly for primary call-to-actions (e.g., "Book Your Expedition"), active calendar dates, alerts, and critical booking status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#DFD7C9`, Warm Sand):** Used for elevated secondary cards, filter bars, trip itinerary badges, and subtle callout backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#1F2421`, Volcanic Basalt / Soft Black):** Deep, warm charcoal used for body typography, primary high-contrast structural borders, and robust dark-mode surfaces. Avoids harsh `#000000` in favor of warm mineral depth.
- **Canvas Base (`#F9F9F6`, Off-White Chalk):** Warm, calming paper base that reduces glare under direct sunlight when users browse on mobile devices while traveling.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic levels to guarantee crisp legibility under bright outdoor conditions and high scanability for international tourists. 

The typeface pairs robust structural geometric shapes with humanist warmth, reinforcing the business's duality between tactical 4x4 ruggedness and genuine Banyuwangi hospitality. 

Heading treatments leverage deliberate tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with hefty weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor editorial narratives, tour highlights, and safety advisories. Body text remains roomy with a relaxed `1.5` to `1.6` line-height ratio, guaranteeing fatigue-free reading across detailed tour itineraries and gear preparation checklists.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a disciplined, open 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to showcase sweeping photography alongside clean itinerary data:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with a max content container width of `1280px`, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2.5rem` outer margins. Content spreads out to let photography breathe alongside booking widgets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Sidebars collapse gracefully into stacked full-width segments.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` side margins. Tour itineraries collapse into vertical timeline blocks, and checkout bars pin cleanly to the viewport bottom.

Rhythm is guided by an 8pt architectural scale. Spacing tokens prioritize clarity and breathing room: generous vertical card padding prevents booking forms and expedition feature lists from feeling cramped.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic glass layers, harsh box shadows, and blurred gradient backdrops. Depth is established through earthy physical planes, subtle surface tinting, and organic, natural shadow dispersals.

- **Flat / Base Layer (`level-0`):** The primary canvas surface (`#F9F9F6`).
- **Surface Cards & Itinerary Modules (`level-1`):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Sand (`#EDE8DF`) cards featuring a structural `1px` low-contrast border in `#E5E0D5`. Supported by an organic soft shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Interactive Popovers (`level-2`):** Used for jeep selection drawers, date pickers, and gear checklist dialogs. Uses an ambient drop shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08)`, paired with a solid 1px border (`#D5CFC3`).
- **Sticky Actions & Mobile Navigation (`level-3`):** Pinned conversion bar uses `0 -4px 20px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06)` with a crisp top divider to separate actions from scrolling tour copy.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a "Soft" corner language (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are modest and utilitarian, echoing tailored canvas bags, reinforced 4x4 expedition gear, and clean architectural maps rather than bubbly consumer apps.

- **Buttons, Inputs, and Badges:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius for purposeful, sturdy utility.
- **Tour Detail Cards & Modals:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, preserving a tidy, grounded silhouette without feeling cold or brutalist.
- **Hero Visuals & Media Modules:** `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) max radius, framed with hairline `#DFD7C9` borders to treat natural photography like fine prints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background in Sunrise Amber (`#D9822B`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), medium-bold weight, `0.25rem` radius. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover transitions to `#C26E1E` without shifting dimensions.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background in Deep Olive (`#2E3E32`), text in `#F9F9F6`. For secondary exploration links such as "View Route Details" or "Download Packing List".
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background transparent, `1px` border in `#2A302D`, text in `#1F2421`. Hover fills with `#EDE8DF`.

### Cards (Expedition & Route Tiles)
- Rendered with white or `#EDE8DF` surfaces, bordered by `1px solid #DFD7C9`.
- Header spaces integrate an aspect-ratio-locked (16:10) photography frame with subtle image zoom on hover.
- Metadata rows (Duration, Altitude, Difficulty, Vehicle Model) use compact tabular labels with inline icons.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Badges (e.g., "Blue Fire Accessible", "Sunrise Guaranteed"):** Warm Sand (`#EDE8DF`) background, Deep Olive (`#2E3E32`) text, `1px solid #D5CFC3`, uppercase `label-sm` sizing with `0.25rem` radius.
- **Filter Chips:** Off-white background, `1px` border in `#DFD7C9`. Selected state transitions to Deep Olive (`#2E3E32`) with white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5CFC3`, internal padding `12px 16px`, corner radius `0.25rem`.
- Focus state shifts border color to Sunrise Amber (`#D9822B`) with an accessible `2px` subtle outline (`rgba(217, 130, 43, 0.2)`).
- Error state shifts border to rustic terracotta (`#B94A48`) accompanied by inline validation microcopy.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Grounded `18px` structural boxes. Unselected borders use `#A39C90`.
- Selected state fills with Deep Olive (`#2E3E32`) showing an off-white checkmark or central radio dot.

### Specialized Travel Components
- **Vehicle Spec Card:** Showcases the allocated 4x4 troop carrier (e.g., Toyota Land Cruiser FJ40) with passenger capacity, safety cage rating, and driver profile.
- **Itinerary Timeline:** Vertical stepped track in `#DFD7C9` with Deep Olive circular milestone indicators, highlighting time checkpoints (00:30 Pickup, 02:00 Paltuding Basecamp, 03:30 Blue Fire descent).
